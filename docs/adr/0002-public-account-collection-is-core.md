# Treat Public Official Account Collection as the Core Workflow

The desktop product is not limited to backing up the logged-in operator's own WeChat Official Account. It must support selecting target official accounts, including accounts operated by others, and collecting their public articles for local study, reference, and archival workflows; self-archival is included by this broader scope but does not replace it.

**Consequences**

- Account search and target account management remain first-release desktop capabilities.
- The main product flow should center on finding target official accounts, syncing article lists, downloading article content, and exporting Markdown or HTML.
- Language such as "own account backup" should be avoided unless a feature is specifically scoped to the logged-in account.
